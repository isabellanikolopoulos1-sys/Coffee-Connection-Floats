About Cesar Millan

Cesar Millan is a professional dog behaviorst with over 25 years of experience working with the most toughest cases of dogs. He learned the fundamentals of dogs when he lived with grandparnets and saw how his family connected and communicated with the animals he was living with. After years of learning from his family, and at the age 13 he saw a show called Lassie and Rin Tin Tin. He was schocked how those people on tv spoke to a dog and they understood what it meant. Thats where he got the inspriation and motivation. At now the age 21 when he was old enough to leave his hometown Sinola Mexico, goes to California and learn from the best trainers. When he arrived to San diego Califronia with no papers, and didnt speak the english language and was also homeless and lived under a bridge he didnt see it as a hard thing to accomplish because his faith was so high and his mission to learn from Americans how to train dogs was his main focus. Thats where he found a job at a grooming salon and was being an assiatnt to the professional groomers to help tame the dogs who were out control he instinctually knew what to do in those moments. He shocked the people and told him that they wanted him to stay. After working for 3 months at the grooming salon he walked dogs off leash and done that for a while until people noticed how amazing that looked. Thats when he was asked later by people what would you like to do years from now. His answer was "I would like my own Tv show" It was written on the newspaper and a week later at an event he had a line of producers asking what is this show going to be all about. Thats when we was offcially called The Dog Whisperer.

Understanding Energy & Mindsets

What is Energy
To Cesar Millan, energy is everything when it comes to connecting with our dogs and communicating with our dogs. Energy is the universal dog language, they will only follow there pack mates or there human family with good energy like being calm confident which sends an automatic field to your dog that your in control of them and you provide the direction and protection, the dog will automatically follow your leadership because the enegry your projecting is the way they can relate and be familar too. Therefore you become the pack leader that your dog needs. Acccording to Cesar having a confident mindset really gives you and your dog a powerful clear direction. For example, if you have a hard time walking your dog because your dog pulls or tries to reach and bark at other dogs which this most likely happens because your holding the leash short and tense and already worrying about what the dog might do, if you dont know the basics of walking your dog. Cesar says to walk shoulders up head high! So again Cesar pleads to us that having the right energy and feeling confident about the activity your doing with your dog you stay your grounds and you lead your dog the right way. Another key takeway is that dogs will never be able to follow a unstable dog or an unstable human because dogs will feel the unbalanced side of other dogs and there owners. So if we dont use our energy and our mindset correctly they will sense it seconds before you notice yourself not feeling certain about what you do with your dog.

THE THREE PACK POSITIONS

What are the 3 postions in a pack of dogs? According to Cesar its Front of the Pack, Middle of the pack, and Back of the Pack. These postions typically define the senstivity levels of each pack of dogs. So dogs that are front of the pack they are the only ones that are the most powerful and confident. Middle of the pack there life is based on just being happy and there job is to bring joy into the pack. Lastly the Back of the pack they are shy, alert. So when it comes to adopting a dog or fostering one its best to adopt the postion, the breed dont focus on too much. Cesar believes that if you dont pay attention to where in the pack is the dog, the dog will sense your lack of understanding about them, and it will be harder for the person to connect with that dog because the owner didn't educate themselves on what there getting. Cesar teaches us that the best way to know which pack position is the best for you and your lifestyle focus and ask yourself what energy do I have 24/7. Am I low, medium, or high of enegry?. This will help you reflect on what energy your bringing home. Dogs in the wild they depend on eachother by communicating whats going on around them. So these postions in the pack play imporant roles and it makes dog who they are. Every dog has a Spirit, instinct, heart, and they use there mind to always do something productive in their lives.

Follow, Play, Explore!

Cesar Millan goes into depth of the importance of the activites dogs do when they first come into the world. The most important thing to remember for us to learn is how to maintain the structure of how dogs follow, play and explore. Cesar says the reason he defined follow, play, and explore is for people to understand that puppies when there getting to know the world, and there parents is they first follow there Moms, play with there Moms, and explore with there Moms. These activites lead puppies to adolecent to maintain healthy growth and stability when they migrate with there pack and migrate in the world. But, you might come to think of it that when Cesar talks about when we help develop our dogs into the world, we tend not to pratice what the dog needs, or we do the complete opposite. Instead of letting the dog follow there pack, play with there pack, and explore within eachother, we actually do it backwards. Cesar gives us an example of when we walk our dogs and where the dog is in the front and the human is in the back. How is this so? Well, when the human first opens the door the human goes according to Cesar "DO YOU WANT TO GO FOR A WALK"! Cesar even mentions sometimes, this is when the dog first wakes up in the morning, then the dog runs out the door and the human gets pulled and dragged as soon as they start to walk. This is caused by the human not understanding the power of silence and being calm, then when the human opens the door its complete chaos. So Cesar gives us the big picture of what goes on before the person and the dog goes for a walk, but what really suprised him was when the dog is in the front it starts to explore and play with other dogs and gets so excited that when the dog goes back home, to eat he's still has all this energy. So based on Cesar's philososphy how to play these activites correctly with our dogs is to first practice being calm at the door, waiting for the dog to become calm to, leave without saying a word so the dog senses your silence, and by that time when you know the basics of handling the leash, we will get to later, we are leading our dog. Whats so funny is that we don't even realize that half of the problems when we give the dog the chance to follow, play and explore with you everything goes wrong at the DOOR.

"A tool for the body, or A tool for the Mind"

Have you ever wondered why your dog always pulls on the leash, or when you try to recall to your dog on the leash they sometimes wont respond? In Cesar's philososphy he talks about the biggest mistake people make when they are trying to use the leash with there dog, cause it all starts with the question "What leash are you using"? The answer Cesar gets is either anti pull harness, flexi leash, or simple collar with a leash attached. So why do people complain, that there dogs are misbehaving on the leash? Because people only think guiding the dog with its body is the bestway when your dog starts to pull you, and you pull back thinking it will make your dog stop, but in reality Cesar says that when you pull back your basically encouraging your dog to pull and saying "go for it". Cesar tells people the best way to enjoy your walk starts with using the right leash. The slip leash is going to be your life saver. Why? Because it controls the mind, not the body, which you have much more control. The science of the slip leash is that it forces the mind to relax and it gives your dog complete sense of direction because where the leash is placed on the very top of the dogs neck, plus doing the action of pulling up but gently, the dog immedialty stops pulling. One thing to remember as well, Cesar reminds people that the leash isn't the only thing that conveys your dog to listen to you. Without the right enegry your dog wont take you seriously or wont listen, because what you feel takes a trip through the leash making it confusing for your dog to accept clear directions.
